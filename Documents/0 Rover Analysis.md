1. Wheels
Parts: 
a) Rim - lightweight material like Al 
b) Tire - non pneumatic (no air) - resists wear-n-tear
c) Grousers - spiky ridges on tire - can be optimized for braking
d) Axle - rod that holds in place wheel
e) Bearings - low friction balls that help wheel spin

While designing these for mars rover, we must keep in mind — balancing grip, durability, weight, adaptability.
Types:
- Solid Al wheels : e.g., Curiosity rover 
Merits: strong, durable, no risk on puncture 
Demerits: can crack due to impacts, less shock absorption
- Spring steel mesh 
Merits: flexible, better for rocky terrain
Demerits: complex to manufacture, can deform overtime, less rigid
- 3D printed polymer wheels : for prototypes - made using PLA/ABS
Merits: low cost, easily customized
Demerits: low strength, non-durable
- Pneumatic rubber tires : earth-rovers testing - inflated tires like those on cars/bikes
Merits: great shock absorption, smooth ride on various surfaces
Demerits: can’t be used in space, punctured easily, heavy
- Modular/ hybrid wheels
Merits: adaptability to different terrains, easy repair
Demerits: mechanically complex, heavy, hard to build

2. Chassis
Skeleton of rover - holds all components in place
Handles -static load(weight of components), dynamic load(stress from rocks/bumps),torque, vibration
Best material - Aluminium 6061 - light, strong
Design goals - non-breakable, stable, light
